# Vaccine Immune Response Research

**Research portfolio | Krithika Anand | Biotechnology**

A high-level overview of laboratory experience contributing to a study of immune responses following vaccination. This repository highlights research methods and skills while excluding participant information, detailed experimental procedures, and unpublished findings.

## Research focus

Investigating cellular immune responses and biochemical markers associated with vaccination through cell-based experiments and laboratory analysis.

## Research experience

- PBMC isolation, cell counting, and preparation for cell-based assays.
- Peptide-stimulation experiments to investigate cellular immune responses.
- Sample preparation for downstream cytokine and cellular analysis.
- Biochemical assessment of oxidative stress and protein damage markers.
- Spectrophotometric measurements and structured recording of observations.
- Data organization and analysis using GraphPad Prism.

## Workflow at a glance

Sample preparation → cell-based stimulation assays → biochemical measurements → data organization → comparative interpretation

This is a conceptual overview rather than an experimental protocol.

## Skills demonstrated

PBMC handling · Cell-based assays · Peptide stimulation · Spectrophotometry · Oxidative stress assays · GraphPad Prism · Scientific documentation

## Interpretation and scope

Immune-response and oxidative-stress measurements represent different aspects of biological response. This portfolio overview reports no study outcomes, statistical comparisons, or clinical conclusions.

The repository contains only this public-facing summary. It excludes the source document, participant demographics and health information, cohort details, sample identifiers, exact protocols, reagent quantities, raw or processed measurements, statistical results, and internal research records.
